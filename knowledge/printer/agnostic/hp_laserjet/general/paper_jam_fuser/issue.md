# Paper Jam in Fuser

# Printers (Hardware Troubleshooting)

Use this guide to instruct a user on how to safely clear a physical paper jam located in the rear heating element (fuser) of an HP LaserJet without damaging the device.

### Paper Jam Fuser

## Severity:
`S2` — High impact; the printer is completely halted and will not process any jobs for any users until the physical blockage is cleared.

## Symptoms
- The printer screen flashes orange and displays an error code beginning with 13 (e.g., 13.B9.D2 Fuser Jam).
- The printer stops mid-job, and the edge of a piece of paper is visible sticking out of the top output bin.
- The device emits a crinkling paper sound before halting.

## Quick checks
- Warn the user heavily: The fuser operates at around 400 degrees Fahrenheit. They must not touch the metal rollers or use sharp tools like scissors or letter openers to dig for the paper.
- Open Tray 2 (the main drawer) to ensure the paper isn't simply folded over at the pickup roller before checking the rear.


## Fix steps
1. Walk to the back of the physical printer and open the rear door (usually a drop-down panel).
2. Look for the jammed paper. If it is caught in the fuser rollers, look for two green or blue release levers on the left and right sides of the fuser unit and press them down to release the pressure.
3. Grasp the paper with both hands and pull slowly and evenly towards you. Do not yank, or the paper will tear and leave tiny scraps inside the gears.
4. Once removed, snap the green/blue pressure levers back into place and close the rear door securely.
5. The printer screen should automatically say "Clearing path" and spit out a blank page to reset the mechanism.

## Escalate if
- The paper tears, and a scrap is completely inaccessible, requiring a technician to dismantle the fuser.
- The paper is cleared, all doors are closed, but the screen still insists there is a jam in the fuser (indicates a broken optical sensor flag).
- The printer jams on every single page fed through it, crumpling the paper like an accordion.

## Ticket fields to capture (when escalating)
- Printer Model: (e.g., HP Color LaserJet M452nw)
- Exact Error Code: (e.g., 13.B9.A2)
- Torn Paper: (Did a piece rip off inside? Yes/No)
